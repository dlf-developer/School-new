# DLF Schools Workspace Customization Rules

## Rule 1: Scope Lockdown

- **Locked Components/Sites**:
  - Do NOT modify any components, routes, styles, or logic specific to **Master Group Website** / Portal.
- **Active Focus**:
  - Focus exclusively on the **DLF Public School (DLPS)** (Sahibabad) and **DLF World School (DLWS)** (Greater Noida) components, sections, pages, routes, and logic.
