# DLF Schools Workspace Customization Rules

## Rule 1: Scope Lockdown

- **Locked Components/Sites**:
  - Do NOT modify any components, routes, styles, or logic specific to **DLF Public School (DLPS)** (Sahibabad) website/portal.
  - Do NOT modify any components, routes, styles, or logic specific to **DLF World School (DLWS)** (Greater Noida) website/portal.
- **Active Focus**:
  - Focus exclusively on the **Master Group Website** components, sections, pages, routes, and logic.
